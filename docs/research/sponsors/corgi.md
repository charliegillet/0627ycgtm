# Corgi - Sponsor Dossier (AI Growth Hackathon, June 27-28)

**TL;DR:** "Corgi Cafe 24/7," the coffee sponsor, is **Corgi Insurance** (`corgi.insure`), a YC S24 AI-native, full-stack insurance carrier for startups. The café is literally the 24-hour coffee shop on the ground floor of their SF HQ at 9 Claude Lane. Corgi is one of the buzziest (and most controversial) startups of 2026: roughly $268M+ raised, a $2.6B valuation as of June 2026, and an aggressive, in-person, founder-led sales culture. Their "growth engineer" / GTM team is a textbook DETECT -> ENRICH -> SCORE -> ACT outbound machine built on HubSpot. That makes them an unusually on-theme sponsor for a growth-engineering hackathon.

## What Corgi actually is

- **Company:** Corgi (legally Corgi Insurance). Website `https://www.corgi.insure/`. Tagline: *"Business Insurance at the Speed of Compute"* / *"Startup Insurance, Quoted in Minutes."* Logo is a corgi dog.
- **What they do:** A **full-stack insurance carrier** (not a broker). They underwrite and issue policies directly, controlling underwriting, policy design, and claims in-house. They describe themselves as building "AI financial infrastructure for the next hundred years."
- **Product lines:** Directors & Officers (D&O), Commercial General Liability, Tech Errors & Omissions, Cyber, Employment Practices Liability (EPLI), Media Liability, Fiduciary, Hired & Non-Owned Auto, AI Liability, Reps & Warranties. Packaged by stage: Pre-Seed/Seed -> Series A -> Growth -> Custom.
- **Who they sell to:** **Venture-backed tech startups.** Vertical focus: SaaS, AI, fintech, marketplaces, health-tech. Named customers include **Deel** and **Artisan**. Expanding into new verticals starting with **trucking**.
- **The application flow (this is the growth-relevant part):** Self-serve online app in ~5 minutes, instant quote, same-day binding, no broker, no mandatory sales call. Data collected: trailing-12-month + projected revenue, optional financials, and **funding amount + date raised**. Pricing for pre-seed/seed "starts around a few hundred dollars a month."

## The "Corgi Cafe 24/7" connection (the hackathon coffee sponsor)

- Opened **Feb 13, 2026** on the retail floor below Corgi HQ. Open 24/7, 8 baristas at $20/hr, specialty lattes, $14 smoothies.
- Origin: Laqua was *required* to lease the retail space as part of the office lease, and built a café instead of subletting.
- **It is explicitly a growth/recruiting play.** Laqua framed it as "the watering holes on the African savannah" where investors scout talent. Corgi gives **YC alumni a 20% discount** to drive foot traffic from their target customer base (YC founders). They plan to replicate it in their other office cities. It runs at a loss ("we are losing some money on it").

## Growth / sales team (most decision-relevant)

Corgi's growth org maps almost perfectly onto the hackathon's DETECT -> ENRICH -> SCORE -> ACT frame. From their open roles:

- **Founding Growth Engineer** (zero-to-one, works with leadership): "build and own the **outbound + CRM infrastructure** that drives pipeline." Responsibilities: build/scale **HubSpot + outbound systems**, workflows/automations that generate consistent leads, **source and enrich high-quality outbound lists**, **clean and structure data** to scale, test tools fast. Wants strong hands-on HubSpot/CRM, prior outbound/lead-gen system building, technical enough to automate/integrate tools, execution-first, in-person.
- Other live roles: **GTM Associate**, **Founding Head of Sales**, **Founding Team Member - Sales**, **RevOps (HubSpot & Sales Enablement) - London**, Strategy & Research Associates, Account Executives.
- ~**10 BDRs in London**; "launching 7 companies" with traction on the startup-insurance program. Sales talk directly to startup CEOs/operators.
- **GTM philosophy:** product-led + aggressive outbound. Yuan: *"People will just go to the easiest, most convenient product available."* Moat is regulatory (2 years, $80M+ to build a carrier).

**Natural custom signal for Corgi's own GTM:** *a startup just raised a round.* A funding event means a new entity with new D&O/cyber/EPLI exposure and fresh cash, i.e. a perfect-timing insurance buyer. Their own app even asks "amount raised and date." Funding-announcement detection -> enrich (stage, headcount, vertical, existing coverage) -> score (fit + urgency) -> act (personalized outbound + instant quote link) IS Corgi's growth motion.

## Reputation / culture (read the room before pitching)

Corgi is famous *and* polarizing. Useful context so we don't accidentally praise the wrong thing:

- **Intense culture:** Laqua publicly advocates 7-day work weeks ("productivity scales with hours"), drew heavy backlash. Employees reportedly got company-logo tattoos and were given mattresses to sleep in the office.
- **Litigious:** issued cease-and-desist letters liberally, including to a **coffee-shop founder (Hello World Cafe)** who merely *joked* on X about the Papermark dispute.
- **June 2026 "vibe-coding" controversy:** Papermark (open-source data-room software) accused Corgi of copying its Dataroom product word-for-word. Laqua said *"no code was used"* but admitted they "vibe-coded design," copying visuals/feature language from competitors, and updated it.
- **Valuation skepticism:** $1.3B -> $2.6B in roughly three weeks raised eyebrows.

Takeaway for the demo: Corgi people will be impressed by **fast, scrappy, technically real outbound/growth automation** and by anything that smartly turns a custom signal (especially funding/risk events) into pipeline. Avoid leaning on their controversies as a joke in the pitch.

## Idea hooks for the hackathon

1. **"Just-raised" insurance/expansion radar (Corgi's own motion):** Detect new funding announcements (Crunchbase/news/SEC Form D/X) -> enrich the company (stage, headcount, vertical, tech stack, whether they already have coverage) -> score fit + buying urgency -> auto-draft hyper-personalized outbound + an instant-quote/onboarding link into HubSpot. This is *exactly* what their Founding Growth Engineer is hired to build; demoing it to Corgi judges is a direct hit on judging criterion #1 (GTM usefulness).
2. **Custom risk-signal scoring for any vertical:** Generalize the above into a "signal-to-pipeline" engine where the user picks a trigger event (funding, hiring spike, new product launch, breach/incident, new entity formation) and the system wires DETECT->ENRICH->SCORE->ACT. Lands the Lopus thesis ("higher-leverage signals are custom, not bought").
3. **HubSpot outbound co-pilot / list-builder:** Agent that sources + enriches + dedupes + structures outbound lists and writes the HubSpot workflow, mirroring the Founding Growth Engineer JD. Strong "technical complexity" story if it does real enrichment + scoring, not just a CSV.
4. **Café-as-CRM (coolness angle):** Riff on the Corgi Café idea, a "physical-signal" growth tool, e.g. capture/qualify in-person founder leads (QR -> instant enrich -> score -> follow-up), turning a watering-hole into a measurable pipeline source. High coolness, ties literally to the coffee sponsor.
5. **Founder-fit underwriting/qualification agent:** Given a company URL, auto-assemble the risk/fit profile a carrier or any B2B seller needs (revenue signals, funding, headcount, exposure) and produce a quote/score in seconds, echoing Corgi's 5-minute application.
