## Orange Slice: what it is and the origin story

**Product:** an **agentic sales-enrichment spreadsheet where every column is TypeScript that
AI writes for you.** You describe a column in chat ("find the CEO's LinkedIn and get their
email"), it generates code, creates the column, and runs it across thousands of rows. Ships
a fully-typed SDK with **100+ enrichments**. Positioned against **Clay / Apollo / SyncGTM**
as the **code-first, technical-GTM-engineer** option ("removes the abstraction layer").

**Origin thesis:** Kishan ran a growth experiment at **Ramp** and learned that wins come from
**finding the right customers at the right time, not better copy.** Vihaar's version: *"the
biggest lever in sales isn't what you say - it's who you say it to."* So Orange Slice scans
**custom, industry-specific signal sources** - court dockets for legal tech, building permits
for proptech, freight listings for logistics, news, job posts, social - and **scores**
prospects by buying intent. This is exactly the hackathon's DETECT → ENRICH → SCORE → ACT frame.

**Funding / stage:** **$5.3M seed** post-Demo-Day, co-led by **1984 Ventures** and **Moxxie
Ventures**, with **Paul Graham** as an angel (YespRESS). Claims **5,000+ sales/RevOps teams**;
early logos cited: Oracle, Confido Health, Pirros, Glass Health. (Treat user/logo numbers as
marketing.) Hiring a founding engineer. Team is small (2 founders + early hires).

## His public POV on GTM / the "AI growth engineer" (this is the gold)

He posts constantly. Recurring, quotable beliefs - align demos to these:

1. **"GTM is no longer about execution - it's about signal + timing + thinking."** He sees a
   power-law splitting "people who are operating systems for demand" from "people who are just
   sending messages." Average GTM disappears; the top ~1% capture the upside.
   (LinkedIn: "the GTM space is about to get hit by power laws")

2. **"The biggest lever isn't what you say - it's who you say it to."** Targeting > copy.
   (YC launch)

3. **Custom signals beat bought signals.** *"We can do this for any signal. Think of companies
   that have a CEO that ran track at Harvard. Or a VP of sales that only has four fingers."*
   Absurd-on-purpose examples to make the point: the leverage is in *novel, bespoke* signals.
   (American Bazaar)

4. **Outbound/cold email is dead; attention is the bottleneck.** Email is free, so "everyone
   sends everything → nothing matters." He argues **friction creates value** - a $0.02 Venmo
   pitch got him to read the whole message because it cost something. Future = paid messaging,
   gated networks, new channels. (LinkedIn: "Email is Broken, LinkedIn is the New Bottleneck")

5. **The future of automation is NOT no-code, and NOT autonomous agents - it's "code-based
   subagent orchestration."** Visual builders are too rigid; fully-autonomous agents are too
   unreliable; the answer is **deterministic code that orchestrates scoped LLM subagents.**
   (Orange Slice blog, Mar 2026) - this is his core engineering aesthetic.

6. **He builds on Claude Code himself.** He shipped **"GTM Claw,"** a workflow library turning
   **OpenClaw + Claude Code** into a "customer-finding machine" (find people talking about your
   problem on Reddit/LinkedIn/X → identify buying-signal companies → enrich decision-makers →
   ICP-check → push to sequence/CRM). Internal uses: scrape LinkedIn reactors on competitors'
   posts, detect product complaints on Reddit, spot operators switching jobs, surface companies
   hiring GTM engineers. He also published a **"Cold Email Writing Skill for Claude Code."**
   (X/LinkedIn; gtmclaw.app)

7. **People use AI sales agents as a "fast GTM decision engine, not a static workflow builder."**
   He's interested in *interactive, iterative* GTM reasoning, not fixed pipelines. (OS blog)

## What impresses him / how to make him say "I'd use this Monday"

- **Pick ONE weird, custom, hard-to-buy signal and wire DETECT → ENRICH → SCORE → ACT end to
  end.** Show it firing on *real* data live (a real Reddit thread, a real permit feed, real
  LinkedIn reactors), not a mock. The "four-fingered VP of sales" energy: a signal nobody sells.
- **Close the loop to ACT.** He cares that qualified leads actually land in a sequence/CRM/Slack.
  A detector that doesn't drive an action is half a project to him.
- **Code-first, deterministic orchestration of scoped LLM subagents** - not a no-code toy and
  not one big "do everything" agent. Match his "code-based subagent orchestration" thesis.
- **Show it's leverage, not theater.** Tie the demo to a number: pipeline created, hours saved,
  cost-per-qualified-lead. He's anti-gimmick; "just do Y."
- **Lean into friction/attention or virality** if you touch outreach - he thinks free outbound
  is dead. Novel channel or earned-attention mechanic > another cold-email generator.
- **Open-source it cleanly on GitHub** (required) and demo on Claude Code / agentic tooling - he
  personally ships on that stack and will respect it.
- **3-min video, working demo, no slides.** Energy and a real "whoa, it actually found that"
  moment beats polish.

**Anti-patterns that will bore him:** another Clay clone, a no-code drag-and-drop builder, a
cold-email copy generator, a dashboard with no action, or a slide-heavy pitch with a faked demo.

## Idea hooks for the hackathon

- **"Four-fingered VP" signal engine:** let a user describe *any* bizarre custom signal in
  natural language → it auto-assembles the DETECT→ENRICH→SCORE→ACT pipeline (code-gen scoped
  subagents) → fires on live data → pushes scored leads to Slack/CRM. Mirrors OS's own pitch but
  as an open, generalizable builder. Maps to track 3 (Reading Minds).
- **Competitor-reactor harvester:** scrape reactors/commenters on a competitor's LinkedIn/X
  posts → enrich → ICP-score → draft a *friction-respecting* outreach (gated/paid/novel-channel)
  → queue. Directly echoes his own GTM Claw use case + his "attention bottleneck" thesis.
- **Churn/intent "mind-reader":** agentic analytics over product + support + Reddit/forum chatter
  that surfaces accounts about to churn or about to expand, with a one-click save/expand play.
  Track 3, and very "fast GTM decision engine."
- **Virality engine:** an algorithm-hacking tool that finds in-market conversations and spins
  compliant UGC at scale (track 6) - leans into the "astroturfing/virality" taste he flagged.
- **Friction-as-a-feature outbound:** a paid/gated micro-message channel (the "$0.02 Venmo"
  idea productized) that proves cost-to-send raises reply rates. Contrarian, on-thesis, demoable.
- **Build it on Claude Code subagents** and open-source it - speaks his exact engineering and
  tooling language and differentiates on Technical Complexity.
