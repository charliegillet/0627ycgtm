## Bottom line (decision guide)

Treat the EV decimals as rough tiers, not precision: a 3-word rubric (useful, hard, cool) scored by
2-3 people in a noisy room cannot tell 83.4 from 77.2. The honest output of all this work is not a
single number, it is a clear shape, and a real disagreement worth reading (the synthesizer ranks
Protagonist #1; the devil's advocate gives it a NO-GO). Here is the reconciliation we stand behind.

**What the judges told us, to our faces, that they reward:**
- ONE custom, hard-to-buy signal (NOT bought feeds), wired DETECT to ENRICH to SCORE to ACT, live.
- Retention / personalized lifecycle / context-memory is the underrated frontier.
- Cold email is dead (~1% reply). The live edges are IRL, events, and consumer channels.
- The single most-endorsed demo move (both judges plus the moderator): go talk to a REAL operator in an
  unfamiliar industry on camera, ask "how do you find customers, can I build you a pipeline," build it,
  and put the recording in the submission.

**The four calls:**

1. **The highest-leverage move is independent of which idea you pick: shoot the 3-minute video as a
   recorded, in-person, real-operator session.** Almost no team will do it, and it is exactly what the
   judges asked for. Lock a real operator by Saturday ~9pm or record a strong self-demo fallback. It is
   bolt-on-able to any idea, so it is a multiplier on whatever you build, not a tiebreaker between ideas.

2. **Pick the idea by the ONE thing you can make genuinely fire live on real data, not by the prettiest
   concept.** A flawless small demo beats an ambitious broken one under "cool = useful + fast." Non-negotiables
   for whatever you build: at least one signal must actually fire live (do NOT claim realtime over a
   hardcoded dataset to judges who build this for a living and will read your open-source repo); the scoring
   must abstain honestly (Danylo's trust thesis); and the ACT must NOT be a generated cold email (Vincent:
   email is dead) - route a qualified human + dossier, or do a non-email / IRL action.

3. **The two builds worth your time:**
   - **Defector, reframed** (the red-team's strongest survivor). The only genuinely custom, hard-to-buy
     signal in the set: detect the public moment a company rips out a competitor, then run a win-back
     (retention, which both judges elevated). Critically it does NOT invite Vincent's lethal "this is a
     worse version of what I already build" reaction. Caveats: GitHub dependency-removal alone is brittle
     and narrow (most B2B competitors leave no GitHub trace), so choose the ONE public rip-out signal you
     can fire live and un-staged, make the ACT a non-email play, and build the abstention/lineage for real.
   - **Protagonist, only if salvaged.** Its judge-as-protagonist wow is memorable, but as specced it
     re-implements Vincent's own Chat GTM in front of Vincent, its "three custom signals" are three bought
     feeds AND-ed together, and its climax is the dead email motion. Keep its genuinely good parts (the
     Convex realtime spine + abstention/lineage) but replace the bought composite with ONE constructed
     behavioral signal (Danylo's "developers frustrated with their IDE" archetype is the template) and make
     the ACT non-email. Do that and it stops being a Chat GTM clone and becomes defensible.

4. **Your passion play: ClubRadar (running).** If you want to build something you care about and also
   seriously contest the Convex $1k, this is the pick (not NBA Backboard, not music BreakRadar): smallest
   theme tax, safest public data (deliberately no Strava API, which now bans AI use), best Convex showcase.
   Honest ceiling is #4 to #6 for the main prize because "running" pattern-matches as consumer in a RevOps
   room. Build it for conviction + the side prize, with the recorded run-club-operator demo as its
   differentiation, ONLY if you have a real run-club organizer or running-store owner already in hand to
   film. Do not spend your top-3 swing on it expecting to beat the field.

**Decisive one-liner:** build a single genuinely-custom-signal, Convex-native app with honest abstention
and a non-email ACT, and shoot the demo as a recorded real-operator session. Defector-reframed is the
cleanest path to top-3; ClubRadar is the pick if you want a passion + Convex-$1k swing.

### Brain
*An Obsidian-style, agent-navigable company brain so your GTM agents finally know what your data means.*  (EV 56.6: top-3 55 / Convex 62 / genuine-use 65)

Danylo's literal stated thesis: data consolidation is solved by warehouses; the NEW problem is teaching AGENTS which company data matters and how the company works. His bet is an Obsidian-style NESTED FILE SYSTEM of linked metrics/definitions that agents navigate ('agents are very good at file systems'), the 'company brain', and the data team shifts from ad-hoc answers to maintaining this system. Brain builds exactly that, scoped to GTM. It is a versioned, linked markdown knowledge graph of governed GTM definitions (what 'qualified', 'MRR', 'churn', 'ICP', 'expansion-ready' mean), each definition linking to its source tables, its SQL/logic, its owner, and related metrics. A GTM agent navigates this filesystem to answer questions and act, and crucially it REFUSES (abstains) when a question requires a definition the brain doesn't contain or can't join, citing exactly what's missing, rather than hallucinating. Editing a definition propagates to every downstream answer. It is the governed semantic layer made into a file system agents can read and a human can maintain.

- **Judge fit:** Danylo's outright bullseye and his most technical idea space: company-brain / context-engineering / Obsidian-style nested linked metrics is the thing he stood up and said is the meatiest problem and his bet. It is his company's core IP (self-healing semantic layer, governed definitions, refuse-rather-than-hallucinate, show-your-work) reframed as the agent-navigable thing he says is the NEW frontier. Vincent overlap: agent-native, 'agents managing agents', non-engineer-usable; and it is the substrate every GTM agent (incl. ChatGTM) needs.
- **Signal:** Not a lead signal but a meta-capability: a natural-language GTM question or agent action whose required definitions/joins are either resolvable from the brain (act) or NOT (abstain + name the gap). The 'custom' part is the company-specific governed definition graph itself.
- **Loop:** DETECT: Bootstrap the brain by scanning the warehouse/CRM schema + sample data and proposing draft definitions (LLM), which a human accepts/edits into linked markdown nodes in Convex. Detect 'answerability': given a query, the agent traverses the linked definitions to find the needed metrics/joins. | ENRICH: Each definition node is enriched with: source tables, the exact SQL/logic, owner, confidence, and links to related/derived metrics (the nested graph). Self-healing: when a schema changes, the agent flags affected nodes and proposes regenerated logic from the plain-English definition (Lopus's signature move). | SCORE: Answerability + trust score per query: can the brain resolve every needed definition with valid joins? This is the 'most dangerous tool is one that always answers' principle made literal. | ACT: Two ACT modes: (1) answer a GTM question with full lineage, and (2) power a downstream GTM agent (e.g. 'score these accounts as expansion-ready') using the governed definitions so the action is grounded and explainable. - **Stack / Convex:** Convex (the linked-definition graph as reactive documents, version history, reactive propagation of edits to downstream answers; novel use of Convex as a live knowledge graph + the $1k angle) + OpenAI structured outputs (definition drafting, query planning over the graph, abstention) + DuckDB/Postgres for the actual metric SQL execution (name-checks Danylo's stack) + optional dltHub framing for ingestion.
