### Reactive Ray (Convex Track Optimizer)

> I win one prize: the $1,000 "best use of Convex," and I judge every idea by one test - if you delete Convex, does the app collapse? The winning pattern is making realtime reactivity the literal star of the video: two browser windows side by side, a signal lands, scores recompute, a draft streams token-by-token, an AE in the other window claims it - all without a single line of websocket plumbing - then we cut to the Convex dashboard and the judge watches rows mutate live. That cut is the move that has won this track before, and I will design the demo around it. So I refuse to back anything where the AI lives in a separate Python service and Convex just logs results. The winning idea must be a live, multiplayer signal war room where the pipeline runs end-to-end on real data in under 60 seconds, names its components out loud, and proves reactivity with two windows. Everything else is a feature, not a winner.
