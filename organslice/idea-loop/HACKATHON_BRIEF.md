## The six tracks
1. **Sales Cyborgs** — AI that augments human sellers (research, outreach, call assist, deal intelligence).
2. **Ad Factories** — AI that mass-produces and tests creative/ad assets.
3. **Reading Minds** — AI that infers intent/psychographics from behavior, conversations, or public signal.
4. **Revenue Autopilot** — AI that runs revenue-driving workflows with minimal human input (pricing, dunning, expansion, lifecycle).
5. **Zero to One** — AI that creates a net-new product/wedge or a brand-new GTM motion.
6. **Algorithm Hacking** — AI that games/optimizes distribution algorithms (SEO, social, app store, recommendation, virality).
